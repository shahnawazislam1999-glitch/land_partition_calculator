How to use

  • Type the land in bigha, kata, lessa. You can fill one box or all three.
  • Set how many people will share the land.
  • Tap Calculate to see one person's share in bigha, kata and lessa.

Example

3 bigha divided among 4 people: 3 bigha = 300 lessa, 300 ÷ 4 = 75 lessa each = 0 bigha, 3 kata, 15 lessa.

If the land does not divide evenly, the share shows lessa with decimals (for example 16.67 lessa).
